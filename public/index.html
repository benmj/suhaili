    How to play

    14 June 1968. You are Robin Knox-Johnston, alone in the 32-foot ketch Suhaili, bound round the world without stopping. Sail 30,000 miles from Falmouth, south of the Great Capes, and back. Historically it took 312 days.

    • Each turn is 4 days. Set a sail plan, rations and a watch task, then sail on.
    • Sail: Full is fast but wears the ship. Reefed is steady. Heave-to is for storms.
    • Rations stretch the stores but wear down body and mind.
    • Tasks mend the ship, catch food and rain, rest you, or bring news over the radio.
    • Spares are canvas, wood, tallow and line. Repairs use them. You never touch land.
    • Rivals (Moitessier, Tetley, Crowhurst, and others) share the sea. Their fates are as in 1968.

    You lose if the hull founders, if you starve, or if your spirit breaks. Tap the chart to switch between the regional view and the whole world.
